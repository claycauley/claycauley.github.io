What Drives Me

The Principles Behind
The Work

Good work doesn't happen by accident. These are the principles that guide every decision I make — from the first sketch to the final line of code.

Design with Intention

Every color, typeface, and pixel has a job to do. I don't add things because they look interesting — I add them because they serve the user.

Built to Last

I write code I'm not embarrassed to look at six months later. Clean, documented, and structured so the next person isn't starting from scratch.

People First

Accessibility isn't a checklist — it's respect. I build things that work for everyone, regardless of how they're accessing the web.

Always Iterating

The best work rarely comes from the first idea. I embrace feedback, test assumptions early, and treat every project as a chance to get sharper.
