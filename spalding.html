Spalding.com

E-Commerce Brandsite Redesign

For Spalding, we took their existing brand guide and elevated it into a full design system that gave their digital presence consistency and flexibility. We mocked up the site to bring the new look to life, then built out reusable ReactJS components to ensure efficiency and scalability. Along the way, we migrated their entire store to a new e-commerce platform, streamlining both the backend and customer experience. The result was a modern, future-proof site that feels as bold and dynamic as the brand itself.
