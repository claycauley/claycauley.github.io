Hello.
My name is Clay.

I'm a full-stack designer from Kentucky.

I love turning ideas into experiences. From crafting clean, thoughtful interfaces to building the underlying functionality that makes them work, I live for every step of the process, blending creativity with problem-solving. Each step has its own unique challenges, and I love applying a diverse set of skills to solve them.
